The principle

Groups are temporary. Knowledge is permanent.

Medical students rotate through units constantly. MedLink is built around that rhythm rather than fighting it.

01
You join the units you're taking this semester and share notes with the people sitting the same CAT.
02
The semester ends and the group archives. Nobody has to maintain a dead WhatsApp group forever.
03
Everything useful shared inside it moves into the permanent library, for the year below you.
