Quick First Aid Protocols

Apply direct, firm pressure with a clean cloth or gauze. Elevate the wounded limb if possible. Keep constant pressure until medical help arrives. Do not remove soaked cloths—layer new ones on top.
Stand behind the person, wrap arms around waist, make a fist above the navel, grasp fist with other hand and give quick upward thrusts until airway clears.
Cool the burn with gentle, cool running water for at least 15-20 minutes. Do NOT apply ice, butter, or toothpaste. Cover loosely with sterile film or clean plastic wrap.
